# MindLore full-video gap: confirmed cause and repair

Owner asked why no full video appeared for nine days and requested repair plus confirmation of scheduled videos. A strictly read-only authenticated YouTube audit on November 19 Chicago time found no full-length video with a future publishAt. Its latest public full was published at 00:00 UTC November 11, i.e. November 10 Chicago time. Existing old private videos remain private; nothing was rescheduled or published by the audit.

The two weekly full-video builds were succeeding but repeatedly generating script 448-5. The saved push trigger contains that exact script and the workflow read it even for scheduled and empty manual runs. The builder then overwrote its unused-script selection with the requested ID, bypassing its own built history. Repeated reviews (including the latest November 18 eight-minute-31-second video) were skipped as duplicate titles. Publisher state marked older uploads done although an earlier November 11 audit had explicitly unscheduled them. The approved queue therefore had no pending entries; job success did not mean a new public video.

Repair: only an actual push event may read the push trigger. Scheduled and default manual runs choose eligible unused story scripts. Explicit requests also reject built IDs and previously built/approved titles; invalid requests fail instead of silently selecting something unrelated. Existing history and review decisions are preserved. No deduplication, approval, factual-quality or publishing gate is disabled. No Shorts workflow or account setting changes.

Six offline regression tests pass, covering fresh series ordering, built-ID replay rejection, duplicate-title rejection, approved titles, legacy/missing scripts and scheduled-trigger isolation. A read-only selection against current repository data found two fresh candidates, 15154-5 and 55262-5. This does not prove either rendered output passes quality checks. A fresh build must still complete review/approval before an actual YouTube release can be scheduled. No promise of a publishing time is made.
